definition: >-
  The process by which greenhouse gases in Earth's atmosphere (such as water vapor, CO₂, CH₄) absorb outgoing
  infrared radiation emitted by Earth's surface and re-radiate thermal energy in all directions, warming the lower atmosphere.
symbol: null
unit: null
equation: null
common_misconceptions:
  - The greenhouse effect functions like a physical glass greenhouse preventing convection (the atmospheric effect operates primarily by absorbing and re-emitting infrared radiation).
  - The natural greenhouse effect is harmful (without the natural greenhouse effect, Earth's average surface temperature would be approximately -18 °C).
learning_objectives:
  - Explain energy balance between incoming solar radiation (shortwave) and outgoing terrestrial radiation (longwave/infrared).
  - Identify major greenhouse gases and their relative warming potentials.
  - Distinguish the natural greenhouse effect from the enhanced greenhouse effect caused by human activities.
real_world_applications:
  - Global climate modeling and carbon footprint accounting.
  - Renewable energy policy and emission reduction technologies.

## Notes

Atmospheric greenhouse gases absorb longwave infrared thermal radiation, stabilizing surface climate.
