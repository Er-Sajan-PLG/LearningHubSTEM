  The unifying geological theory explaining that Earth's rigid outer shell (lithosphere) is divided into several
  large plates that move slowly over the asthenosphere, driven by mantle convection currents.

common_misconceptions:
  - Continents float on liquid ocean magma (tectonic plates consist of rigid lithospheric rock moving over ductile asthenosphere).
  - Tectonic plate movement is fast and noticeable daily (plates move at rates of 1–10 cm per year).

  - Explain mantle convection as the primary thermal driving mechanism for plate motion.

## Notes

Plate interactions shape ocean basins, continental landforms, and volcanic arcs over geological timescales.
