name: Force

definition: >-
  An influence that can change the motion of a body. In classical mechanics the net force on a
  body equals the rate of change of its momentum (F = dp/dt).
symbol: F
unit: newton (N)
equation: F = dp/dt
common_misconceptions:
  - A constant net force produces constant speed (it produces constant acceleration).
