common_misconceptions:
  - Energy is a substance (energy is a property of systems, not a material).
  - Energy can be "used up" (energy is conserved; it only changes form).
  - Only moving objects have energy (stored energy — potential — exists without motion).
  - Heat and temperature are the same as energy (heat is energy transfer; temperature is a measure of average kinetic energy).

## Notes

Energy is the most conserved and most universal concept in physics. It connects mechanics,
thermodynamics, electricity, and nuclear physics into a unified framework. Understanding that
energy is a number we can calculate — not a substance — is essential scientific literacy.
