name: Displacement
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The change in position of an object, measured as a straight-line vector from the initial position
  to the final position. Displacement has both magnitude and direction, making it a vector quantity.
symbol: Δx or s
unit: metre (m)
equation: Δx = x_final - x_initial
common_misconceptions:
  - Displacement is always equal to distance (they differ when the path curves or reverses).
  - Displacement depends on the path taken (displacement depends only on start and end points).
learning_objectives:
  - Define displacement as a vector quantity.
  - Calculate displacement from position data.
  - Distinguish displacement from distance in multi-segment motion.
real_world_applications:
  - Navigation systems (shortest-path calculations).
  - Physics of projectile motion.
  - Engineering (structural displacement under load).

## Notes

Displacement is the bridge between geometry and motion. It is the foundation for velocity and acceleration as vector quantities.
