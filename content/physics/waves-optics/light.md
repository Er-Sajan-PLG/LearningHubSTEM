definition: >-
  Electromagnetic radiation that is visible to the human eye. Light behaves both as a wave and as
  a particle (photon), though at Grade 10 the wave model predominates.

equation: c = f·λ (c = speed of light in vacuum ≈ 3×10⁸ m/s)
common_misconceptions:
  - Light needs a medium to travel (light travels through vacuum).
  - Light travels instantaneously (it has finite speed).
  - Light is only what we can see (visible light is a small part of the EM spectrum).

  - Describe light as a form of electromagnetic radiation.
  - State the speed of light in vacuum.
  - Explain that light can travel through vacuum.
  - Distinguish between luminous and non-luminous objects.

  - Vision.
  - Fiber optics.
  - Solar energy.
