type: quantity
name: Frequency
domain: physics
status: draft
definition: >-
  The number of complete wave cycles passing a given point per unit time. Frequency determines
  the pitch of sound and the color of light.
symbol: f or ν (nu)
unit: hertz (Hz)
equation: f = 1 / T (T = period)
common_misconceptions:
  - Higher frequency means larger waves (higher frequency means shorter wavelength).
  - Frequency depends on the medium (frequency is determined by the source; wavelength changes with medium).
learning_objectives:
  - Define frequency and its unit (hertz).
  - Calculate frequency from period and vice versa.
  - Relate frequency to wave speed and wavelength.
