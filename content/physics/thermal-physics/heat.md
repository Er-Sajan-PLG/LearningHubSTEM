name: Heat
domain: physics

definition: >-
  Energy transferred from one body to another due to a temperature difference. Heat is energy in
  transit — it is not stored in an object (the stored energy is internal energy).
symbol: Q
unit: joule (J)
equation: Q = m·c·ΔT (specific heat) ; Q = m·L (latent heat)
common_misconceptions:
  - Heat and temperature are the same (heat is energy transfer; temperature is a measure of average kinetic energy).
  - Heat is stored in an object (heat is energy in transit; internal energy is stored).
  - Cold is a form of energy (cold is the absence of heat; heat flows from hot to cold).
learning_objectives:
  - Define heat as energy transfer due to temperature difference.
  - Distinguish between heat and temperature.
  - Calculate heat transfer using specific heat capacity.
  - Explain why objects reach thermal equilibrium.
real_world_applications:
  - Cooking (heat transfer to food).
  - Climate and weather (heat transfer in atmosphere).
  - Engine cooling systems.
  - Insulation in buildings.
key_experiments:
  - Measuring specific heat capacity of a metal using calorimetry.
  - Observing heat transfer between hot and cold water.

## Notes

Heat is one of the most misunderstood concepts in physics. Clarifying that heat is energy transfer
(not a substance, not stored) is essential for understanding thermodynamics.
