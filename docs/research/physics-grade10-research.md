## Curricula Researched

### 1. Nepal CDC — SEE (Grade 10)
Physics portion of Science curriculum. Key topics: Units/measurement, motion, Newton's laws, work/energy/power, heat, light (reflection/refraction/lenses), sound, electricity/magnetism (Ohm's law, circuits, electromagnetism), gravitation, pressure, fluids, momentum, machines, basic atomic/nuclear, energy sources.

### 2. CBSE India — Class 9-10 (Science, Physics portion)
Motion (distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration, equations of motion), force and Newton's laws (inertia, momentum, action-reaction), gravitation (universal law, free fall, weight, thrust, pressure, Archimedes, relative density), work and energy (kinetic, potential, power, conservation), sound (propagation, reflection, echo, ultrasound, SONAR), thermal properties (heat, temperature, specific heat, change of state), electricity (current, potential difference, Ohm's law, resistance, heating effect, power), magnetic effects (magnetic field, force on conductor, electromagnetic induction, motor, generator), sources of energy.

### 3. UK England — KS4 GCSE Physics (AQA/OCR)
Energy (stores, transfers, conservation, efficiency, specific heat capacity, power), particle model of matter (density, states, internal energy, specific latent heat, gas pressure), atomic structure (isotopes, ions, nuclear radiation, half-life, fission, fusion), electricity (charge, current, potential difference, resistance, series/parallel, power), magnetism and electromagnetism (fields, electromagnets, Fleming's rule, induction), forces in balance (vectors, scalars, resultant forces, free-body diagrams, Newton's laws, equilibrium), motion (speed, velocity, acceleration, graphs), force and motion (momentum, conservation, impulse, stopping distance), wave properties (transverse, longitudinal, EM spectrum, reflection, refraction, sound), light (reflection, refraction, lenses, color), radioactivity (alpha, beta, gamma, half-life, uses), astronomy (solar system, stellar evolution, Big Bang).

### 4. US NGSS — Grades 9-12 (through Grade 10)
Forces and interactions (Newton's laws, momentum conservation, gravitational/electric/magnetic forces), energy (conservation, kinetic/potential, thermal energy, transfer, systems), waves and EM radiation (wave properties, EM spectrum, information transfer), structure and properties of matter (atomic structure, molecular bonding), stability and instability, electric and magnetic forces (fields, induction), nuclear processes (fusion, fission, decay), wave properties (mechanical and electromagnetic).

### 5. IB MYP — Science (Physics strand, Years 4-5 ~ Grade 9-10)
Forces and motion (Newton's laws, momentum, energy transformations), energy (conservation, transfer, efficiency, power), waves (properties, light, sound, EM spectrum), electricity and magnetism (charge, current, circuits, induction), thermal physics (temperature, heat, specific heat, change of state), atomic and nuclear physics (structure, radioactivity), space physics (cosmology, solar system, stellar evolution).

## Recurring Concepts (Union across curricula)

These concepts appear in ALL or nearly all curricula and form the canonical core:

1. Measurement, units, and physical quantities
2. Motion: distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration
3. Graphs of motion (distance-time, velocity-time)
4. Forces and Newton's laws of motion
5. Momentum and conservation of momentum
6. Work, energy, and power
7. Conservation of energy
8. Gravitation
9. Pressure and atmospheric pressure
10. Density, buoyancy, and fluids (Archimedes' principle)
11. Heat, temperature, and thermal expansion
12. Heat transfer (conduction, convection, radiation)
13. Change of state and specific heat/latent heat
14. Wave properties (transverse, longitudinal, frequency, wavelength, speed)
15. Sound (production, propagation, reflection, echo, resonance)
16. Light (reflection, refraction, lenses, mirrors)
17. Optical instruments (microscope, telescope)
18. Electric charge, current, potential difference, resistance
19. Ohm's law and electrical circuits (series/parallel)
20. Electrical power and energy
21. Magnetism (magnetic fields, electromagnets)
22. Electromagnetic induction and applications (motor, generator)
23. Atomic structure (Bohr model, isotopes, ions)
24. Radioactivity (alpha, beta, gamma, half-life)
25. Nuclear processes (fission, fusion)
26. Energy sources (renewable and non-renewable)
27. Machines and mechanical advantage (levers, pulleys, efficiency)
28. Scientific measurement, uncertainty, and experimental method

## Canonical Scope Decision

**Domain:** Physics (phys)
**Grade boundary:** Through Grade 10 / end of secondary / KS4 equivalent
**Total canonical concepts:** ~70-80 entities (covering the 28 recurring areas above)

The canonical model is organized as:

```
domain: physics
  ├── unit: measurement-and-quantities
  │     ├── concept: physical-quantity
  │     ├── concept: unit
  │     ├── concept: si-system
  │     └── ...
  ├── unit: motion
  │     ├── concept: distance
  │     ├── concept: displacement
  │     ├── concept: speed
  │     ├── concept: velocity
  │     ├── concept: acceleration
  │     └── ...
  └── ...
```

This taxonomy is **curriculum-independent**. Curricula map onto this knowledge system; they don't define it.

## Depth by Grade 10

By end of Grade 10, a learner should be able to:

- **Understand** the core principles (Newton's laws, conservation laws, wave behavior, circuit basics)
- **Calculate** using key equations (F=ma, KE=½mv², V=IR, p=mv, W=Fd, etc.)
- **Predict** outcomes in simple systems (what happens if mass doubles, if resistance increases)
- **Explain** phenomena using physics principles (why objects float, how lenses form images)
- **Interpret** graphs (distance-time, velocity-time, heating curves)
- **Design** simple experiments and analyze results
- **Connect** physics to real-world applications (energy sources, machines, optics)
